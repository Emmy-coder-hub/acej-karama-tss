About Our School

ACEJ Karama TSS, located in Muhanga District, is committed to providing quality education for all students. We focus on academic excellence, character development, and extracurricular growth.

Our mission is to nurture talented, responsible, and knowledgeable students who can excel in Rwanda and beyond.
