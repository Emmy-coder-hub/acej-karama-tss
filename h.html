About ACEJ KARAMA TSS

Located in Muhanga District,Muhanga Sector,Nganzo Cell. this is school that offer skills,knowledge,displine and technology

Vision

The general vision of acej karama tss in Rwanda focuses on the following:
-Universal Quality Education: Providing equal opportunities for all citizens to access high-quality education through world-class learning facilities.
-Empowerment and Skills: Equipping youth with necessary skills and tools to become the next generation of skilled, compassionate leaders and problem solvers.

Mission

The general mission of acej karama tss in 2026 includes:
-Human Capital Development: Transforming students into skilled human capital to drive the socio-economic development of Rwanda.
-Equitable Access: Ensuring all learners have fair access to quality education, regardless of their background.

Core values

  • Integrity: Doing what’s right, even when unseen.
  • Excellence: Striving for high standards in all we do.
  • Service: Giving back to our community.
  • Innovation: Embracing creativity and technology.
  • Respect and Tolerance: Promoting a culture of peace and mutual respect for others’ rights and diverse backgrounds.

Admissions

Welcome applications of acej karama tss arounding the following NIT,CSA and ACCOUNTING. Our team is here to guide every step.

Requirements

  • Academic records: Previous transcripts/report cards.
  • Identification: Passport photo and basic biodata.
  • Placement:Trade/program preference.
  • Fees: Non-refundable application fee.

Process

  1. Submit: Online application or in-office form.
  2. Review: Academic evaluation and interview.
  3. Offer: Admission letter and next steps.
  4. Enroll: Orientation and class placement.
